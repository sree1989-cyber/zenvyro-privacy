Game progress on your device

The game stores completed levels, best move counts, and sound preferences on your device. No account is required. We do not operate a game server or upload your puzzle progress to our own server. Android backup is disabled in the supplied configuration. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove local progress.

Advertising services

The Android source includes Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs. Development uses Google test ad units. Advertising SDKs can collect and share IP address, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. IP address may be used to estimate location. Actual practices depend on SDK versions, configuration, consent, and jurisdiction.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising controls.

Your choices

Where required, a consent form is presented before ads are requested. A privacy-options entry is available in Settings when required by Google's SDK. Rewarded ads are optional and initiated by the player. Puzzles can be played offline; advertising requires a connection. Local data can be cleared through Android's application storage settings.

Intended audience

Traffic Escape: Parking Logic is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, and is not intended for children under 13. This audience statement is not an official content rating. Applicable age-related advertising and consent requirements must still be verified before release; a 13+ audience does not mean that every player is an adult.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until it is cleared, the app is removed, or storage is otherwise reset. Advertising data is governed by Google's applicable terms and retention practices. Contact support@zenvyro.tech with privacy questions.
