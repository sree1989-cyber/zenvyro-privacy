1. About this policy

This policy explains how information is handled in Traffic Escape: Parking Logic, including its puzzle and driving features. No game account is required. The game includes Google advertising and consent technology; an absence of visible ads is not a statement that no third-party information processing occurs.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, completed levels, best results, sound preferences and an advertising age-group choice on your device. The age choice is stored separately from puzzle progress. We ask for an age group, not a date of birth. We do not operate a game-progress server or upload your saved puzzle results to our own server. Android backup is disabled in the app configuration.

Game sound effects are bundled with the app and played locally. Microphone recording is not used to produce these effects. Your Sound setting and device media volume control playback.

3. Google advertising and consent services

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on their operation, configuration and your choices, these services may collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, other identifiers where applicable, app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic/performance information. An IP address may be used to estimate a general location even though the game does not request GPS location permission.

Google uses information under its applicable terms and policies, including for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its service providers may process information outside your country. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising controls for details about Google's processing and choices.

4. Age groups and advertising eligibility

The game is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older, subject to applicable local requirements, and is not intended for children under 13. This intended audience is separate from the game's official content rating.

App-initiated ad requests are limited to players who confirm they are 18 or older and satisfy the applicable consent requirements. The app does not initiate ad requests when the age group is unknown, under 18, declined or unavailable because of an error. Adults must confirm again in each new app session. You can change the age choice in Settings. The choice is self-declared, not an identity or age-verification service.

This app-level restriction controls ad requests; it is not a guarantee that native Google SDK components never initialize or process startup or previously initialized SDK information before an age choice is made. Puzzles and hints remain available without advertising.

5. Privacy choices and optional ads

For eligible adults, the app checks Google's consent permission before app-initiated SDK initialization and ad loading. Applicable consent messages and privacy options depend on region and the Google service configuration. A privacy-options entry is offered in Settings when Google's SDK requires it. You can use the available controls to review or change your choices.

Rewarded hints require a deliberate choice to watch an ad. Ads are not required to save game progress. Puzzles can be played offline; advertising requires a connection. Android settings also provide advertising-ID controls. Certain advertising uses may qualify as sale, sharing or targeted advertising under local privacy laws; use the applicable privacy choices and Google controls for those uses.

6. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and handle the reported issue. Please do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data remains until it is cleared, the app is removed or device storage is otherwise reset. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove achievements and saved progress. This does not itself delete information already processed by Google.

Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its policies; we do not set a fixed retention period for Google's data or promise that we can delete it directly. Support correspondence is handled through our support mailbox. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or objections to particular uses of information. Contact support@zenvyro.tech for privacy questions or requests. For information controlled by Google, its privacy tools and request channels may also be needed.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our information practices change and revise the effective date above. The current public policy is available at this policy page.
